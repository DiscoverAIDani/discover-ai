Meet Danielle

AI Communicator & Coach

My goal is simple: help people realize what a powerful and helpful tool AI can be.

I don't believe you need to become “a tech person” to use AI. You already know how to talk to another person— you can start there.

We'll work from what you want or need, then work backwards. If AI gives you too much, we'll simplify it. If it gets something wrong, we'll correct it. If you don't know what to ask, we'll ask AI to help us figure that out.

Your curiosity is the starting point.
