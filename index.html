Community Program

Free AI Education for Your Community

Discover AI is designed to help community organizations introduce adults to AI in a relaxed, practical, beginner-friendly way.

The goal isn't to turn people into technology experts. It's to help them leave thinking, “Maybe I can actually use this.”

FREE 60-MINUTE COMMUNITY INTRODUCTION

A relaxed introduction for adults who are curious about AI but may feel intimidated by technology.

  • Start with YOU
  • Talk naturally with AI

“Same Questions. New Possibilities.”

01

Start With What You Care About

We don't begin with a lecture about technology. We begin with something people actually want to do, learn, plan, understand, or create.

02

Ask AI Like You'd Ask a Person

There isn't one magical way to phrase a question. If AI doesn't understand, explain what you meant and try again.

03

Make the Answer Work for You

Ask AI to summarize, simplify, give examples, change the format, or focus on just one part.

04

Keep Exploring

Once you understand the conversation, the possibilities become much bigger than a single demonstration.

Questions

Getting Started Is Simple

Do I need AI experience?

No. Discover AI is designed especially for beginners.

Do I need to know the right prompts?

No. We start with natural conversation and learn how to steer from there.

Who is this for?

Adults, seniors, community members, and anyone curious about AI who wants a comfortable place to start.

Does my organization have to be technical?

Not at all. Discover AI is designed for ordinary community settings.

Is the community presentation free?

Yes. Discover AI's community introduction is offered as a free educational program.

What happens after the introduction?

Participants can continue exploring AI on their own using the ideas and skills introduced during the session.
