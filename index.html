About Discover AI

Meet Danielle

I'm Danielle Wilcott, founder of Discover AI.

I created Discover AI because AI shouldn't feel like something that belongs only to programmers, technology professionals, or people who already understand it.

My goal is to help everyday people discover practical ways AI can make life a little easier — while also learning how to use it thoughtfully and responsibly.

Discover AI is currently focused on building connections with local community organizations and bringing beginner-friendly AI education directly to the people they serve.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

A few quick answers about the Discover AI community program.

Is Discover AI currently free?

Yes. Discover AI is currently offering free beginner presentations to community organizations as it builds local partnerships and expands access to practical AI education.

Do people need to know anything about AI beforehand?

No. The presentation is designed for complete beginners. No technical experience is required.

Who can request a presentation?

Community organizations, senior centers, nonprofits, libraries, learning groups, civic organizations, and other groups interested in helping their community understand AI are welcome to reach out.

What does my organization need to provide?

A space for the presentation and the opportunity to invite your community. Discover AI provides the presentation and educational materials.

Can you tailor the presentation to our group?

Yes. When appropriate, examples can be adjusted to fit the interests and everyday needs of the people attending.

What if I'm an individual who wants to learn about AI?

You're welcome to reach out. Discover AI is currently focused on community presentations, and connecting with individuals helps identify what kinds of AI education would be most useful locally.
