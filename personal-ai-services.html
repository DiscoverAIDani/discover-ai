Clear, Comfortable Boundaries

Your Security Comes First

Please do not email passwords, account recovery codes, payment details, or other sensitive information. You enter passwords yourself. Discover AI does not store customer login credentials or access banking, medical, or other sensitive accounts on your behalf.

Meeting & Travel

Public libraries and community centers are preferred meeting locations. Discover AI serves Citrus Heights and nearby communities, including Folsom, El Dorado Hills, and Rocklin. Travel within the local service area is included; longer-distance travel fees are discussed before booking. Home visits may be available by special arrangement.

Payment & Changes

Each 60-minute session is $65. Payment is cash only for now, collected at the appointment before instruction begins. A receipt is emailed afterward. If you need to cancel or reschedule, 24 hours' notice is preferred. There is no cancellation fee.
